About the Candidate

Born Here. Built Here. For Here.

I am Odera Odhiambo — a son of this ward, a product of its schools, a witness to its struggles, and a believer in its enormous potential. I grew up watching our mothers walk kilometres for water, our youth graduate with no jobs to return to, and our elders grow old without the dignity they deserve.

I am running for MCA of Kinanie Ward not to have a title, but to sit in the Machakos County Assembly and ensure our ward's voice is the loudest in the room. I understand the law. I know exactly what an MCA can and cannot do — and I will use every legal tool available to transform Kinanie Ward.

The Plan

Our Manifesto: 6 Key Promises

Concrete, legal, achievable commitments — tracked and held accountable on the Transparency page.

01

Kinanie Ward Enterprise Fund

Propose a Bill to establish a dedicated ward-level enterprise fund — grants and low-interest loans for youth and women businesses. Every eligible business in Kinanie will have access.

🎯 Employment
02

Modern Kinanie Market

Lobby the Machakos County government to build or fully renovate our ward market — covered stalls, clean water, proper drainage, and electricity. No more trading in the rain.

🏪 Infrastructure
03

County Bursary Transparency

Fight for a fair, transparent bursary system where every qualifying student in Kinanie gets their county allocation — published publicly so every parent can verify the list.

📚 Education
04

Boda Boda Welfare Bill

Propose a Machakos County Boda Boda and Transport Welfare Bill — designated stages, an insurance scheme, safety gear subsidies, and full legal protection for every rider.

🏍️ Transport
05

Kinanie Health Access

Ensure every resident can access a functional health facility within 3km. Push for a fully-staffed MCH unit, free maternal kits, and mental health services at our health centre.

🏥 Health
06

Monthly Ward Report

Every last Friday of the month, I will publish a written report to Kinanie Ward: Assembly votes, what I'm fighting for, what has been delivered — no disappearing acts.

🔍 Transparency
